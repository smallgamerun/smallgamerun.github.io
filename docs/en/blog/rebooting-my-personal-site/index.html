Rebooting My Personal Site

More than a visual refresh: turning a dormant template into a public workbench I can maintain.

  • Meta
  • Web

Two years ago, I launched this site with an academic website template. It did the job of giving me a homepage, but it never became a place where I consistently documented my work or thinking.

This rebuild starts with subtraction. The fictional papers, résumé entries, and sample posts that shipped with the template are gone. What remains is deliberately grounded in work and information I can verify. The new structure is simple: projects answer what I have built, while the blog records how I think.

Why restart now

There is a rich set of problems at the intersection of Financial Engineering, quantitative research, AI, and software engineering. Instead of waiting until everything feels complete, I would rather build a public workbench that can improve over time.

Future updates will add project retrospectives, learning notes, and a fuller account of my experience. They may not arrive on a fixed schedule, but each should be honest, clear, and useful to revisit.
